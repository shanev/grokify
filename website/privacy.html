Privacy policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

In short: Grokify does not collect personal data. It has no servers of its own, runs entirely in your browser, and saves only two settings.

Overview

This policy explains the data practices of the Grokify browser extension ("the Extension").

Data collection

Grokify does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to us or to anyone acting on our behalf. The Extension does not:

  • Collect your browsing history
  • Track your activity
  • Store personal information
  • Send data to servers operated by Grokify (there are none)
  • Use cookies or tracking technologies
  • Share data with third parties

Settings storage

The Extension uses Chrome's storage API (chrome.storage.sync) to save two preferences:

  • Enabled: whether the Extension is turned on.
  • Mark replaced links: whether rewritten links show a small "G" mark.

If you're signed in to Chrome, Chrome syncs these preferences across your browsers. They stay within Google's infrastructure and are not accessible to us.

Link rewriting

The Extension changes Wikipedia links on web pages to point to grokipedia.com, and records the original address on the link so it can show it when you hover. This happens entirely in your browser; nothing about the pages you visit is sent anywhere.

Country flags on X

When you view a profile on X (x.com or twitter.com), the Extension asks X for that account's "Account based in" information so it can show a country flag. The request goes directly to X, from the X page you're already on, using your existing X session. It is the same kind of request X's own site makes. No data from it is sent to us or to anyone else, and results are kept only in memory while the tab is open.

Permissions

  • storage: to save the two preferences above.
  • activeTab: to reload the current tab when you turn the Extension on or off.
  • Host access (https://*/*): to find and rewrite Wikipedia links on the pages you visit, and to show country flags on X profiles.

Third-party services

The Extension does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
